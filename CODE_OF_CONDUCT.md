# Community Conduct

Participation must remain useful, evidence-led and safe.

## Expected

- Discuss claims rather than attacking contributors.
- Mark spoilers clearly.
- Correct mistakes with evidence.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Keep reports specific enough to reproduce.

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, threats or discriminatory conduct.
- Deliberate misinformation or fabricated evidence.
- Posting personal data, credentials or private correspondence.
- Piracy links, malicious files or instructions to bypass platform security.
- Unmarked major spoilers used to disrupt discussion.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions that breach these rules and may restrict repeat offenders.
