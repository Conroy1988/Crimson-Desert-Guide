## Mount layers

Crimson Desert uses mounts as a toolkit rather than one upgrade ladder.

| Layer | Best starting use |
|---|---|
| Regular horses | Roads, settlement circuits and predictable overland travel |
| Special mounts | Terrain-specific travel, combat utility and species-specific handling |
| Legendary mounts | Spoiler-controlled traversal with special access conditions |

## Special mounts

Patch 1.06.00 allows certain animals to be registered after gaining their trust.

Eligible species:

- Bear
- Boar
- Wolf
- Deer
- Mountain Goat
- Kuku Bird
- Iguana
- Raptor
- Camel
- Lion
- Tiger

Ferocious animals can be subdued and fed. Some species require unique methods. Feeding an untamable animal does not increase trust.

## Feeding and registration

1. confirm the species is eligible;
2. carry enough food and inventory space;
3. subdue the animal when required;
4. feed only in the correct interaction state;
5. verify that trust increases;
6. stop spending food if it does not;
7. complete registration before leaving;
8. test summon, dismissal and quickslot behaviour safely.

A registered special mount can be fed while riding by selecting food in the inventory and choosing **Feed**.

## Quickslots

The mount quickslot supports:

- one regular mount;
- one special mount.

Check the regular and special tabs separately before assuming a mount is missing.

## Saddles

Special-mount saddles are separate equippable items rather than part of the default appearance.

| Saddlery | Saddles |
|---|---|
| Hernand | Wolf, Bear |
| Equinsher | Deer, Wolf |
| Calphade | Bear, Kuku Bird |

## Later special mounts

Patch 1.10.00 enabled Baby Wyverns and Kuku Bird Chicks as special mounts. Patch 1.11.00 allows Wyverns to be mounted with Axiom Force without learning Back Hang.

## Choosing by task

| Task | Starting choice |
|---|---|
| Settlement circuit | Regular horse |
| Long road route | Horse or understood fast special mount |
| Narrow or rough terrain | Smaller or terrain-suited special mount |
| Hostile route | Mount with known combat and dismount behaviour |
| Vertical traversal | Unlocked aerial special mount |
| Long aerial traversal | Spoiler-controlled legendary mount |

Test the same route under the same conditions before declaring one mount universally faster.

## Blackstar spoiler boundary

Blackstar is treated as a legendary aerial traversal mount. Official patch history confirms a Breath action and fixes involving death state, water pathing and performance. The guide does not yet publish an unverified unlock sequence, universal summon rule, exact Breath damage or a confirmed armour set.
