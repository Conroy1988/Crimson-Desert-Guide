[h1]17. Collectibles, Knowledge & Challenges[/h1]

[b]Guide baseline:[/b] Patch 1.15.00  
[b]Evidence rule:[/b] An achievement category confirms the category goal, not its unpublished total, every individual entry, coordinates, route or reward table.

[h2]Official completion categories[/h2]

The official Steam achievements define category-level goals for:

[list]
[*]Sword, shield, bow, spear, two-handed weapon, rapier and shield, firearm and cannon challenges
[*]Training, battle, operation, mastery and New Power challenges
[*]Abysses, mazes, constellations, sanctums, spires, ancient ruins and secret places
[*]Exploration, trade, Above the Law, Challenges and Changes, minigames and life activities
[*]Special equipment, horses and hunting
[/list]

The guide does not publish a category total unless an official source states one.

[h2]Published category total[/h2]

Patch 1.12.00 added [b]51 knowledge entries[/b] to the [b]Collectibles — Contract[/b] category. The patch notice does not publish all 51 names or acquisition routes, so the guide records the official total without inventing the checklist.

[h2]Named research records[/h2]

Official patch history also names Crescent Lake, Exploding Bismuth Spider, Jarback Crab, Skull Knight Followers, Jared, Darksworn Armor Spectral Soldier, Giant Rock Tusk Warthog, Tristan, Flame Knight, Marni's Machina Knight and Dreadnought. Their existence is official, but the current exact acquisition routes remain research items where the notice does not state them.

Two individual weapon challenges are officially named:

[list]
[*][b]Shield of Unchanging Will VI[/b] — Damiane's shield charge did not count correctly before Patch 1.08.00.
[*][b]Bow Aimed at Fate II[/b] — replenishing arrows did not satisfy the challenge before Patch 1.08.00.
[/list]

Their full objective wording and reward details remain unverified.

[h2]Safe completion method[/h2]

[list]
[*]Read the exact in-game challenge or Knowledge wording before starting.
[*]Record the current patch, character, region and equipment setup.
[*]Change one variable at a time when testing a challenge condition.
[*]Confirm the Journal or Knowledge entry after an interaction, purchase, quest or defeat.
[*]Treat older blocker notes as diagnosis history, not as proof that the defect still exists.
[*]Do not rely on community totals or map coordinates unless independently corroborated.
[/list]

The website catalogue adds filters, spoiler masking, private notes, personal discovery counts, Completion Companion status and validated JSON backup/restore.
