# Pywel Atlas & Route Intelligence

The web guide includes a searchable, local-first Pywel Atlas. It is intentionally a topological register rather than a coordinate map: only verified locations, services and travel-system links are shown.

## Verified travel intelligence

- Patch 1.02.00 added the Pailune Abyss Nexus.
- Patch 1.03.00 made Abyss Nexuses easier to discover.
- Abyss Nexus teleportation can be used while mounted, falling, swimming or climbing.
- The guide does not publish a route between settlements until the connection is directly verified.
- When the atlas cannot prove a path, it returns “No verified route” instead of guessing.

## Progress and privacy

Visited locations, activated nodes and checked services are stored locally in the browser. The atlas supports validated JSON export and import, requires no account and sends no progress to a server.
