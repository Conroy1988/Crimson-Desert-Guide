# Boss Intelligence Centre

> **Major story spoilers:** Steam cannot reproduce the web guide's per-dossier reveal controls. Boss names are shown below.

The Boss Intelligence Centre separates direct official facts from open research. It does not infer weaknesses, phase orders, routes, statistics, rewards or rematch locations from a name, trailer or historic defect alone.

## Identity separation

- **White Horn** is not treated as **Clockwork White Horn**.
- **Awakened Caliburn** is not treated as **Corrupted Caliburn**.
- Shared words, visual similarity or community shorthand are not enough to merge canonical records.

## Evidence boundary

The web guide records every unresolved route, prerequisite, arena, phase, reward and replay field as explicit research work. A current-patch screenshot or video, repeatable start point, ordered observations and completion evidence are required before any dossier is promoted.
