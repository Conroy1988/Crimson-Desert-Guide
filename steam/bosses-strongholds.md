## Rematch rules

- Memory Fragments become active after a boss is defeated.
- Rematches can be started from the previous encounter site.
- Reminisce preserves the original boss state.
- Resonate scales the boss when the player's progression is higher.
- Consumables are restored after a rematch and separate loot is not awarded under the documented Patch 1.05 rules.
