## Regional arrival protocol

When entering a major new region:

1. reveal local map information;
2. identify the nearest Abyss Nexus or usable travel point;
3. mark a storage and resupply hub;
4. inspect the smithy before refining;
5. inspect saddlery stock for the active special mount;
6. check provisioners, equipment shops and requests;
7. leave with a manual save and a known return point.

## Travel network

Abyss Nexuses form the main teleportation network. Post-launch patches expanded the network and allow teleportation in more movement states, including while mounted, falling, swimming or climbing walls where the current situation permits it.

If a Nexus refuses activation, stop combat actions, leave restricted content or dismount before treating it as broken.

## Hernand service hub

Hernand is useful as a reset point between combat, exploration and gathering routes.

| Service | Evidence | Practical use |
|---|---|---|
| Private Storage | Community | Deposit duplicate gear, special materials and excess mount food |
| Smithy | Community | Compare, refine or extract after making a manual save |
| Saddlery | Official | Wolf and Bear special-mount saddles |
| Provisioner's Shop | Official | General provisions; Flower Basket added in Patch 1.11.00 |
| Equipment Shop | Official | Equipment; Ranged Weapons of the World - Bows, Vol. I added in Patch 1.11.00 |
| Requests and vendors | Community | Check local work and specialist stock before leaving |
