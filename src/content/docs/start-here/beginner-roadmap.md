## Intended outcome

The finished roadmap will provide:

- a spoiler-controlled first-session checklist;
- early upgrades with the greatest practical impact;
- systems that can safely be postponed;
- missable or expensive decisions;
- clear hand-offs to detailed system guides.

## Validation required

Each recommendation will be tested on a clean save and rechecked whenever a patch changes progression, rewards, controls or tutorial flow.
