The completion system will be generated from structured data rather than hand-maintained duplicate lists.

## Planned trackers

- quests and optional activities;
- bosses and rematches;
- settlements and points of interest;
- mounts, pets and equipment;
- collectibles and knowledge;
- stronghold and liberation progress.

Progress will be stored locally in the browser and exportable as a small JSON backup. No account or database will be required.
