Crimson Desert uses mounts as a **travel toolkit**, not one linear upgrade ladder. A regular horse remains useful even after special or legendary traversal becomes available because handling, summon access, terrain and combat utility vary by task.

### Regular horses

Use a dependable horse for ordinary roads, settlement circuits and long routes where predictable handling matters more than a specialised movement mode.

### Special mounts

Patch 1.06.00 introduced registration for eligible animals after gaining their trust. Ferocious species can be subdued and fed; some animals require unique methods. Feeding an animal that cannot be tamed does not increase trust.

Eligible species recorded by Pearl Abyss:

### Legendary traversal

Legendary mounts should be evaluated separately because unlock conditions, summon restrictions and story context may be major spoilers. [Blackstar](/mounts/blackstar/) remains behind an explicit reveal control.

## Trust, taming and registration

Use this safe process:

1. confirm the animal is an eligible species;
2. approach with enough food and inventory space;
3. subdue a ferocious animal when the game requires it;
4. feed only after the animal is in the correct interaction state;
5. observe whether trust actually increases;
6. stop spending food if the value does not change;
7. complete registration before leaving the area;
8. test summon, dismissal and quickslot behaviour in a safe location.

:::caution[Unique taming methods]
The official patch notes state that some species require unique methods but do not define every method. The guide will not invent a universal feeding recipe.
:::

## Feeding while mounted

A registered special mount can be fed while riding:

1. open the inventory;
2. select an appropriate food item;
3. choose **Feed**;
4. verify that one item was consumed and the intended value changed.

Patch history includes a defect where feeding could consume two food items on Hard difficulty; that was fixed in Patch 1.11.00. Keep only the quantity needed for the current test until behaviour is confirmed on a new patch.

## Quickslot rule

The special-mount update fixed the mount quickslot so it can hold:

- **one regular mount**; and
- **one special mount**.

Do not interpret a missing third mount as evidence that it has been lost. Check the regular and special mount tabs separately before changing registration or save data.

### Equipment check

Before leaving a saddlery:

1. open the Special Mounts tab;
2. confirm the correct animal is selected;
3. equip the saddle in the mount-equipment interface;
4. verify appearance and statistics;
5. save manually;
6. summon the mount and confirm the equipment persisted.

## Choosing a mount by task

| Task | Starting choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement circuit | Regular horse | Predictable handling and low setup overhead |
| Open roads | Horse or fast registered special mount | Route length matters more than combat utility |
| Rough or narrow terrain | Test a smaller or terrain-suited special mount | Body size and turn radius can matter |
| Hostile route | Mount with understood combat and dismount behaviour | Survival matters more than headline speed |
| Vertical traversal | Baby Wyvern or another unlocked aerial option | Bypasses some ground-route constraints |
| Long aerial traversal | Spoiler-controlled legendary option | Access and summon restrictions must be understood first |

These are operating recommendations, not a universal ranking. Test the same route with the same starting point, weather and loadout before declaring one mount faster.
