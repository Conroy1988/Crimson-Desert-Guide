## Evidence grades

### Official

Directly supported by Pearl Abyss patch notes, notices, guides or in-game text.

### Verified

Reproduced through controlled testing with enough detail for another player to repeat the result.

### Community

Reported independently by multiple credible players but not yet reproduced by the guide team.

### Provisional

A useful lead, hypothesis or incomplete observation awaiting confirmation.

## Required page metadata

Every guide page must declare:

- `currentPatch`;
- `lastVerified`;
- `evidence`;
- `spoilerLevel`;
- `patchStatus`.

The build fails when required metadata is missing or malformed.

## Source discipline

- Paraphrase external material rather than copying it.
- Link to the original source.
- Record test conditions for numerical or mechanical claims.
- Never convert a single anecdote into a confirmed rule.
- Mark uncertainty where the evidence does not support certainty.
