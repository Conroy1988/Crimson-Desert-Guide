The platform foundation is live. Initial pages define the structure and research standard; deep gameplay coverage will be expanded systematically.

## Current operating rules

1. A new official patch triggers an automated review issue.
2. Pages are never silently treated as current after a potentially relevant update.
3. Exact claims require a source or reproducible test.
4. Major spoilers must be isolated and labelled.
5. The website remains the canonical guide; Steam receives a condensed export.
