The encyclopaedia turns canonical Content Archive records into usable operational guidance without promoting names into invented walkthroughs. A record can be useful while its route, reward table, prerequisite chain or boss mechanics remain unresolved.

<GuideDetailCentre />

## Evidence boundary

- **Supported guidance** is restricted to facts already verified by official records, such as Memory Fragment rematch activation and stronghold completion conditions.
- **Limited official detail** records a named objective or historic blocker but does not publish a full sequence.
- **Research required** confirms an official identity while explicitly withholding unknown phases, weaknesses, routes, statistics and rewards.
- Private notes and preparation checks remain in the browser and are never sent to the guide, GitHub or an analytics service.

## Recovery rule

Do not restore an older save merely because an objective behaves unexpectedly. Confirm the current patch, required character, objective state and recorded blocker history first; preserve the newest working save before attempting rollback.
