The guide is **programme-complete**, but Crimson Desert still contains officially named records whose exact route, prerequisite, objective sequence, reward or replay behaviour has not been verified. This centre converts those unknowns into controlled research tasks instead of hiding them or filling them with guesses.

> **Spoiler control:** major story, boss and Abyss identities remain masked until revealed. Use the spoiler filter before browsing if you are still progressing through the story.

<ResearchQueueCentre />

## What counts as usable evidence

A useful submission identifies the current patch and platform, provides a reproducible start point, records the ordered steps, and captures the exact screen or world state supporting the claim. A single observation can be valuable, but it must remain labelled as limited until another controlled run or independent source corroborates it.

## What this centre will not do

- It will not convert a local note into a public guide claim.
- It will not infer rewards, prerequisites, coordinates, totals or mechanics from an activity name.
- It will not publish universal combat rankings or unsupported damage statistics.
- It will not treat an old progression workaround as current guidance without a current-patch retest.

## Where verified findings go

Evidence that survives review is added to the canonical content source, regenerated into the [Quest, Boss & Content Database](/database/), expanded into the [Quest Walkthrough & Encounter Encyclopaedia](/database/details/), and only then becomes eligible for completion tracking where appropriate.
