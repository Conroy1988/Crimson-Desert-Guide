The catalogue distinguishes an official **category completion condition** from a verified **individual item or challenge**. A Steam achievement proves that a category exists; it does not reveal the category's total, every individual entry, their coordinates, or an optimal route.

<CollectibleCatalogue />

## Evidence boundary

- Personal discovery counts and notes are stored only in your browser.
- A user-entered count is never treated as a canonical game total.
- Category records without published totals remain marked **Unknown**.
- Individually named entries are included only when an official patch notice or existing canonical source names them.
- Exact routes, rewards and objectives remain unresolved until supported by an official source or controlled current-patch testing.
