This database deliberately separates **verified completion records** from **research records**. A named quest or boss may be real and officially sourced while its route, reward table or completion condition remains unverified.

> **Need operational guidance?** Open the [Quest Walkthrough & Encounter Encyclopaedia](/database/details/) for preparation, supported checkpoints, blocker recovery, unresolved fields and private local notes.

> **Working on completion categories?** Open the [Collectibles, Knowledge & Challenge Catalogue](/database/catalogue/) for official achievement conditions, individually supported knowledge records, personal discovery counts and research notes.

## How to use the archive

- Use **Verified only** when planning completion work.
- Use **Research records** to see officially named content that still needs route or reward verification.
- Major story and boss identities begin masked.
- Records excluded from the completion percentage remain visible, but cannot be ticked in the Completion Companion.
- Known blocker history records fixed progression defects so an old workaround is not mistaken for current guidance.

## Source policy

The initial catalogue uses official Steam achievement conditions and Pearl Abyss notices. It does not infer exact rewards, optimal routes or prerequisites from a name alone.
