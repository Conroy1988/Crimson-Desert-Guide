Crimson Desert becomes easier to navigate when every new region is treated as an **information and logistics problem**, not merely a road to the next quest marker.

## Regional arrival protocol

Use this order whenever you enter a substantial new area:

1. reveal local map information before committing to a route;
2. identify the nearest **Abyss Nexus** or other usable travel point;
3. mark a safe resupply or storage hub;
4. inspect the smithy before spending materials;
5. inspect saddlery stock if a special mount is part of the route;
6. check provisioners, equipment shops, specialists and requests;
7. leave with a manual save and a clear return point.

This procedure reduces repeated cross-region travel and protects materials from being spent before the local service options are understood.

## Discovery and travel systems

### Bell towers and map information

City bell towers and similar map-information interactions are useful first stops because they reveal regional context and nearby points of interest. Treat revealed markers as **leads**, not proof that every service or reward is immediately accessible.

### Mysterious Energy

A Mysterious Energy location may lead to an Abyss Nexus, an Abyss puzzle or another traversal interaction. Investigate the area before assuming the marker is only decorative.

### Abyss Nexuses

Abyss Nexuses form the principal teleportation network. Post-launch patches expanded the network and relaxed activation restrictions:

- Patch 1.02.00 added an Abyss Nexus in Pailune and improved activation while moving slightly;
- Patch 1.03.00 allowed teleportation while mounted, falling, swimming or climbing walls;
- restored Abyss Skybridge Gates can also function as teleport destinations where available.

:::caution[Travel-state exceptions]
Patch behaviour has changed repeatedly. If a Nexus refuses activation, stop combat actions, dismount or leave restricted content before treating the location as broken.
:::

## Settlement audit template

Record new hubs with the same minimum fields:

```text
Location:
Patch:
Map reveal point:
Nearest travel point:
Private Storage:
Smithy:
Saddlery:
Provisioner/general vendor:
Equipment/specialist shops:
Request board or local missions:
Screenshot or source:
Unverified route notes:
```

A route recommendation remains **community** evidence until its travel time, prerequisites and service availability are recorded on the current patch.
