## Research framework

Combat advice will be divided into reproducible layers:

1. **Inputs** — exact control and timing requirements.
2. **State** — stamina, spirit, stun and animation conditions.
3. **Interaction** — counters, grapples, airborne actions and environmental attacks.
4. **Application** — enemy archetypes and boss-specific decision making.

No damage value, timing window or claimed optimal rotation will be published as fact without repeatable testing.
