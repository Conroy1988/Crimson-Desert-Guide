A build is useful only when its test conditions and trade-offs are visible. This laboratory stores your trials locally and deliberately avoids universal rankings, invented DPS values or cross-character comparisons made under changing conditions.

<BuildLaboratory />

## Controlled-test standard

1. Fix the save, character, equipment state, target, arena and difficulty.
2. Establish a repeatable baseline before changing one variable.
3. Record failures, healing, resource pressure, recovery and terrain—not only successful attacks.
4. Repeat the same trial before increasing confidence.
5. Keep personal results private unless independently corroborated for the current patch.

See [Combat Fundamentals](/systems/combat/), [Weapon & Skill Testing](/systems/weapon-skills/), [Gear, Refinement & Extraction](/systems/gear/) and [Boss & Encounter Diagnosis](/systems/boss-diagnosis/) for the supporting method.
