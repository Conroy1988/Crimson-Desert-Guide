The atlas is a **topological intelligence register**, not a geographic coordinate map. It links only documented locations, services and travel-system edges. Missing coordinates and unverified roads are left unavailable rather than estimated.

<PywelAtlas />

## Reading the atlas

- **Official** records come directly from Pearl Abyss patch notes or Steam achievements.
- **Verified** records are maintained by the guide with an explicit source path.
- A route result uses only the connection graph in the versioned atlas dataset.
- “No verified route” means the guide does not yet have evidence for a reliable path.
- Atlas progress remains in this browser unless you export a JSON backup.

## Current evidence boundary

Patch 1.02.00 added an Abyss Nexus in Pailune. Patch 1.03.00 improved Nexus discovery and teleportation while mounted, falling, swimming or climbing. Patch 1.06.00 published the official special-mount saddlery inventory for eight cities. The official Master Camper achievement names Howling Hill as the camp activation location.

This is enough to build reliable service and system intelligence, but not enough to publish a coordinate-accurate world map or settlement-to-settlement road network.
