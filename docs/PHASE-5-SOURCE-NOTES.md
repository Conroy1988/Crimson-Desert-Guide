# Phase 5 Source Notes

Verified: 25 July 2026  
Guide baseline: Patch 1.15.00

## Official sources used

### Patch 1.02.00

- Added an Abyss Nexus in Pailune.
- Improved Abyss Nexus activation while moving slightly.
- Confirmed restored Abyss Skybridge Gates as teleport destinations.

Source: https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=80&pubDate=20260405

### Patch 1.03.00

- Allowed Abyss Nexus teleportation while mounted, falling, swimming or climbing walls.

Source: https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=81&pubDate=20260411

### Patch 1.06.00

- Added registerable special mounts after gaining trust.
- Listed eligible species: Bears, Boars, Wolves, Deer, Mountain Goats, Kuku Birds, Iguanas, Raptors, Camels, Lions and Tigers.
- Documented subduing/feeding for ferocious animals and unique methods for some species.
- Confirmed that feeding untamable animals does not increase trust.
- Added feeding while mounted.
- Limited quickslots to one regular and one special mount.
- Made special-mount saddles separate equippable items.
- Confirmed saddlery inventories:
  - Hernand: Wolf and Bear;
  - Equinsher: Deer and Wolf;
  - Calphade: Bear and Kuku Bird.
- Confirmed Blackstar Breath as an action through a performance fix.

Source: https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=90&pubDate=20260511

### Patch 1.08.00

- Fixed saddlery comparison tooltips.
- Fixed special mounts being sellable.
- Fixed release actions appearing for unreleasable mounts.
- Increased Iron Eagle trust gained from mercury.
- Added Baby Wyvern as a pet before later mount support.
- Fixed lingering Blackstar Breath effects.

Source: https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=93

### Patch 1.10.00

- Enabled Baby Wyverns and Kuku Bird Chicks as special mounts.

Source: https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=96&pubDate=20260605

### Patch 1.11.00

- Allowed Axiom Force mounting for Wyverns without Back Hang.
- Fixed Blackstar/wyvern water pathing.
- Fixed excessive wyvern land-attack stamina consumption.
- Fixed double food consumption on Hard difficulty.
- Added Flower Basket to the Hernand Provisioner's Shop.
- Added Ranged Weapons of the World - Bows, Vol. I to the Hernand Equipment Shop.

Source: https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-us/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=99

### Patch 1.15.00

- Fixed Wyvern summoning while airborne.
- Fixed a mounted attack restriction on a quest-objective bear.

Source: https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-us/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=109

## Evidence boundaries

The following remain community or provisional:

- exact Hernand coordinates and fastest service circuit;
- complete trust values and food preferences for every species;
- unique taming methods not enumerated by Pearl Abyss;
- universal mount speed or handling rankings;
- Blackstar unlock sequence and repeat-summon restrictions;
- Blackstar exact Breath damage, area and resource values;
- settlement service availability based on story state.

No such claim is promoted to verified without current-patch screenshots or a reproducible test record.
