## First-party project source

Conroy's existing Steam guide:

https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3724081637

Used only for editorial operating principles already authored by the project owner:

- observe, defend, punish and disengage;
- avoid button-mashing and overcommitment;
- defence before damage;
- upgrade equipment that is actually used;
- retain rare or role-specific items;
- recover materials from replaced refined equipment.

These principles are presented as editorial guidance, not as official mechanics or verified numerical claims.

## Claims deliberately excluded

Phase 4 does **not** publish:

- universal best weapons or builds;
- exact animation frames;
- exact damage-per-second rankings;
- undocumented stamina or Spirit costs;
- universal refinement costs;
- boss-specific strategies that reveal identities or story progression;
- socket or resistance claims without a documented current source.

## Future verification templates

The published pages include repeatable templates for:

- weapon and skill testing;
- boss-attempt diagnosis;
- refinement and extraction records.

Numerical findings should be promoted from provisional/community status only after those conditions are recorded and independently reproduced.
