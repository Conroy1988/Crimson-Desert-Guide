## Evidence boundary

The atlas does not include coordinates, scale, distances or inferred road connections. The single route edge is the verified Pailune Nexus link to the abstract Abyss Nexus network. Settlement cards provide service intelligence, not a claim that a direct route between those settlements is known.

## Maintenance

- Stable location, service and connection IDs must not be renamed without a migration.
- New route edges require an official or controlled verified source.
- Unknown routes must remain absent.
- Completion IDs are validated against the generated Completion Companion dataset.
