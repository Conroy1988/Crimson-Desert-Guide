# Phase 15 source notes — Boss Intelligence Centre

Verified: 27 July 2026  
Game baseline: Patch 1.15.00

## Objective

Create one patch-aware intelligence surface for every canonical boss record without converting official names, trailers or fixed defects into unsupported walkthroughs, weaknesses, phase orders, statistics, rewards or rematch locations.

## Canonical official sources

- Patch 1.05.00: Memory Fragments, first-defeat activation, Reminisce, Resonate, consumable restoration and rematch reward behaviour.
- Patch 1.06.00: direct encounter evidence for Queen Spider, Titan, Dreadnought, Storm Crusher, Thunder Crusher, Awakened Caliburn and Myurdin, the Avatar of Umbra.
- Patch 1.07.00: verified Memory Fragment locations for Muskan, Corrupted Caliburn, Goyen, Draven the Crowcaller and Clockwork White Horn, plus Journal discovery guidance.
- Patch 1.13.00: official English rename from Injured Ludvig to One-Armed Ludvig.
- Official boss-battle promotional notice: White Horn, Staglord, Reed Devil and Queen Stoneback Crab identities, explicitly labelled work-in-progress footage.

## Evidence decisions

1. The five Patch 1.07 Memory Fragment records are `verified-rematch` dossiers because both activation and location are officially documented.
2. Titan is `official-rematch` because Patch 1.06 directly names a rematch, but the guide still lacks its Memory Fragment location.
3. A fixed encounter defect can confirm that a mechanic or state existed; it does not prove a weakness, optimal response or complete phase sequence.
4. Promotional identities remain `official-identity` dossiers until their current-release route and behaviour are reproduced.
5. White Horn remains separate from Clockwork White Horn.
6. Awakened Caliburn remains separate from Corrupted Caliburn.
7. One-Armed Ludvig retains Injured Ludvig as a searchable official alias, not a second canonical boss record.
8. Reed Devil's promotional connection to Hernand does not establish the current-release arena or route.
9. Partial canonical records remain excluded from completion totals even when official patch notes add useful mechanic evidence.

## Research requirements

A dossier may only gain route, prerequisite, mechanic, reward, completion or replay detail when the evidence includes:

- the exact current patch and platform;
- a repeatable start point;
- ordered observations or objective states;
- screenshots or video showing the relevant interface or world context;
- completion, reward or Memory Fragment evidence where claimed;
- a clear separation between direct observation and interpretation.

## Maintenance rules

- Every dossier must map to one canonical `boss-*` record.
- Direct facts require approved official HTTPS sources.
- Variant-separation rules must be reciprocal.
- Work-in-progress promotional evidence must retain its caveat.
- Unknown values remain explicit unresolved fields and controlled research work.
- A boss-related patch requires review of the canonical record, Boss Intelligence notes, Research Queue, encounter guidance and Steam export.
