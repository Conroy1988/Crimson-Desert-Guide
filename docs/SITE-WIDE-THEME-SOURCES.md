## Fan-content policy boundary

Pearl Abyss Fan Content Guidelines explicitly permit:

- community sites, blogs and fan pages;
- sharing in-game screenshots or content made using in-game screenshots;
- modifications to game content for compliant fan content;
- free access with no mandatory fee.

The project must remain clearly unofficial. The footer therefore includes the statement:

> This is unofficial content which contains copyrighted materials and IP from Pearl Abyss, and is not official or endorsed content.

Policy source:

- https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-us/Policy?_policyNo=130

## Prohibited asset sources

Do not add:

- community screenshots without the creator's permission;
- scraped artwork from news sites, wikis or social accounts;
- third-party game or franchise assets;
- modified official logos or trademarks;
- fragile hotlinks that are not publisher-controlled.

## Implementation rule

Every external visual URL must be publisher-controlled, documented here, and used only as part of this free unofficial guide. The repository's original CSS, layouts, crest and interface components remain project-owned fan content.
